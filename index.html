Privacy Policy for Mushaf Al-Qur'an

Last Updated: May 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Mushaf Al-Qur'an ("Application") for mobile devices, which was created as a free, offline tool for reading the Holy Quran.

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information or data from its users.

  • There is no account creation or registration required to use the Application.
  • We do not track your location, device identifiers, or usage metrics.

2. Local Data Storage

The Application utilizes your device's local storage solely to enhance your user experience. This includes:

  • Application Preferences: Storing your selected script type preference (e.g., Nastaleeq or Madani).
  • Reading Progress: Storing the "Last Read" page position so you can cleanly resume reading where you left off.

This data is stored strictly on your physical device via standard framework mechanisms (such as Maui Preferences) and is never transmitted to any external servers, third parties, or cloud services.

3. Third-Party Access

Because the Application does not collect any data, no user data is ever shared with, sold to, or processed by third-party services, analytics companies, or advertising networks.

4. Children’s Privacy

The Application is designed for users of all ages, including children. Because the Application collects zero personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with global child privacy regulations, including COPPA and GDPR.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
